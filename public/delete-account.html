Smart Edit account deletion

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Smart Edit and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and profile are deleted straight away.

Can’t open the app?

Email us from the address you signed up with at [add contact email] with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete the account and confirm by email, within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and account ID).
  • Your profile details, such as your display name.

What is not affected

  • Files on your device. They never left it, so you manage them yourself or by uninstalling the app.
  • Anonymous usage statistics already collected, which are not linked to your name or email.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
