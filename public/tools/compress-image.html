Compress images

Make photos smaller so they are quick to send and cheap to store. Pick your images, choose a quality, and download the result.

    Your files never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no sign-up.

    How it works

    1. Choose one or more images.
    2. Pick a format and quality. JPEG at 70–80% is a good balance for photos, and a smaller “longest side” saves the most space.
    3. Tap Compress, then Download next to each result.

    Everything happens in your browser, so even private photos are safe. If a result comes out larger than the original, keep the original: some images are already as small as they can get.
