Merge PDFs

Combine several PDF files into one, in the order you choose.

    Your files never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no sign-up.

    How it works

    1. Choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Use ▲ and ▼ to set the order.
    3. Tap Merge PDFs and download the result.

    Password-protected or damaged files can’t be merged. The tool will tell you which one is the problem. Remove the password in your PDF app first.
