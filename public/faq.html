Frequently asked questions

Is Smart Edit free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads. There are no paid features.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Documents, photos, scans and PDFs are processed on your device. The same is true for the free tools on this website: they run in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

What can the app do?

Scan documents with the live camera, imported photos or the automatic scanner; edit PDFs; convert images and PDFs; compress files; edit images; and read text from pictures (OCR).

Which languages does it support?

The app is available in English, French, Spanish, German, Arabic and Hindi, and follows your phone’s language by default. Text recognition supports Latin scripts as well as Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer.

Why does the app show ads?

Ads keep Smart Edit free. They come from Unity Ads. See our privacy policy for what that involves and how to limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page.

A tool on this site gave me an error. What now?

Very large files can run out of memory on some phones, so try fewer or smaller files. PDFs protected by a password can’t be merged, so remove the password first. If it still fails, contact us and tell us which browser you used.
